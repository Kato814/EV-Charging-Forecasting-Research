# Direction Decision Recommendation — v0.5

## Decision

The current recommended first research direction is:

> **面向在充会话与未到达会话双源不确定性的充电站滚动功率概率预测。**

This is a working description, not a frozen paper title.

## Why this direction is preferred

### It solves a forecasting problem rather than adding a boundary condition

At any rolling issue time, part of the next few hours of power is already partially determined by sessions that are charging now; the rest is generated by future arrivals. Treating both through one historical aggregate sequence discards available state and mixes two different uncertainty sources.

### The nearest neighbors are clear

- remaining occupation prediction already exists;
- partial individual curve prediction already exists;
- future-arrival event models already exist;
- bottom-up probabilistic load forecasting already exists;
- joint occupancy/load prediction already exists.

The research value, if confirmed, lies in integrating these fragments at station level with a legal information set, source-resolved uncertainty and operational evaluation.

### The data path is realistic

- **ACN-Data**: session windows, delivered energy, measured current and pilot signal;
- **DESL-EPFL Level 3**: measured/requested/setpoint power, SOC and control flags;
- optional transaction data: external test for weak-label extensions.

### It connects naturally to later scheduling

The output can provide rolling scenarios for:

- transformer reserve;
- station power-cap enforcement;
- photovoltaic/storage coordination;
- intraday energy procurement;
- congestion and queue preparation.

The optimizer should consume the probabilistic station-power scenarios; it does not need the paper to claim “flexibility prediction” as the first contribution.

## Minimum method architecture to test

### Active-session branch

For every session active at issue time \(\tau\), condition on legally observed information:

- elapsed connection/charging time;
- partial charging-current/power trace;
- delivered energy to date;
- current SOC when available;
- charger/power class;
- pilot/setpoint/control status where available.

Predict the distribution of its remaining duration, energy and power trajectory.

### Future-arrival branch

Predict future arrivals and marks:

- arrival count/time;
- duration;
- requested energy;
- charger or power class;
- conditional power profile.

A marked point process is one option, not a mandatory contribution.

### Probabilistic aggregation

Sample or analytically combine both branches to obtain station-level trajectories. The aggregation must preserve:

- nonnegative power;
- session availability windows;
- delivered-energy accounting;
- charger and site capacity when relevant;
- correlations among sessions and horizons.

### Uncertainty attribution

Report the fraction of predictive variance or error attributable to:

- active-session continuation;
- future-arrival count;
- future-arrival marks;
- charging-control action.

This is essential to show that the decomposition is scientifically informative rather than merely architectural.

## Mandatory baselines

1. seasonal persistence;
2. LightGBM or equivalent tabular model;
3. direct aggregate TCN/PatchTST/TFT-type model;
4. direct probabilistic quantile/density model;
5. bottom-up mixture model similar to `P0065`;
6. compound-Poisson/Cox event model similar to `P0049`;
7. joint occupancy-load model;
8. active-only and arrival-only ablations;
9. one strong generic foundation or diffusion baseline, only as a baseline.

## Mandatory metrics

- MAE/RMSE/WAPE where appropriate;
- CRPS or energy score;
- pinball loss and coverage/width;
- near-horizon error by issue time;
- peak magnitude and timing error;
- ramp-event score;
- threshold exceedance probability;
- scenario-based scheduling cost or constraint risk in a small downstream test.

## Stop conditions

Stop or demote the direction when any of the following occurs:

1. current-session state adds no stable value beyond aggregate history and occupancy;
2. improvement disappears after enforcing strict forecast-time legality;
3. gains are confined to one site or one metric and fail on an independent metered dataset;
4. a simple feature addition to a direct model matches the full decomposition;
5. downstream reserve/capacity decisions show no measurable benefit.

## Parallel routes

- **G02 weak-label power reconstruction**: high novelty, high identification risk;
- **G03 peak timing–magnitude–ramp prediction**: lower-risk fallback;
- **G05 control-aware demand/executed-power separation**: second-stage extension.

## OOD policy

Station or time holdouts may be used to show robustness. They are not the core claim, the title anchor, or the reason for the method.
