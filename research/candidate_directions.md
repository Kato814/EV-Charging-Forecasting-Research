# Candidate Research Directions — Current

The current repository does **not** select distribution-outside generalization as the paper core. Previous v0.3–v0.4 OOD and structural-intervention analyses remain in Git history and archived versioned documents, but they no longer determine the active roadmap.

Current candidates are ranked in [`round3_direction_scorecard.csv`](round3_direction_scorecard.csv):

1. **Primary gate** — active-session and future-arrival dual-source rolling probabilistic station-power forecasting;
2. **Parallel identifiability gate** — weak-label instantaneous power-curve reconstruction and forecasting;
3. **Low-risk backup** — joint peak-timing, peak-magnitude and ramp-event probabilistic forecasting;
4. **Component/backup** — activation, session-count and conditional energy/power joint distribution;
5. **Second stage** — control-policy-aware separation of latent charging need and executed power;
6. **Data gate only** — latent, served and lost demand under censoring;
7. **Supporting module** — multi-resolution temporal consistency;
8. **Exploratory** — interpretable behavior-mechanism mixture experts.
